01
Speaker recognition · 2025–presentIn revision

Speaker recognition based on Mel-frequency cepstral coefficients using machine learning methods

How can a shared audio feature pipeline support a meaningful comparison of neural and classical classifiers?

  • Built block-based preprocessing and MFCC features, including temporal derivatives and spectral descriptors.
  • Compared multilayer perceptron and linear SVM classifiers with ANOVA feature selection and cross-validation.
  • Led data preparation, model development, evaluation, and manuscript writing.

A. Siavashpour, S. Rajabi, and M. Javadian. Manuscript being revised for a new submission.

02
Self-organizing networks · 2024–presentIn preparation

A Comprehensive Survey of Self-Organizing Networks in Next-Generation Cellular Systems

How do cellular networks configure, optimize, and recover autonomously as their scale and complexity grow?

  • Co-authored a survey synthesizing 114 references across self-configuration, optimization, healing, learning, and protection.
  • Compared optimization-based, heuristic, and learning-driven approaches in 4G/5G and emerging 6G systems.
  • Developed comparative tables and technical figures; examined coordination, explainability, and federated learning.

A. Siavashpour, K. Siavashpour, M. Azadegan, B. Safaei, H. Peyvandi, and M. Imran. Manuscript in preparation.
